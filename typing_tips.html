How to type faster

Good habits matter more than moving your fingers quickly.

Give every finger its own keys

Rest your fingers on the home row, the keys with the thick borders, and return there after each key. Use all ten fingers.

  • Pinky
  • Ring
  • Middle
  • Index
  • Thumb

Keep your eyes on the text

Looking down at the keyboard breaks your flow. Let your fingers find the keys by themselves.

Keep a steady rhythm

Each mark below is a key press. Even spacing beats rushing the easy words and then stalling on the hard ones.

Sit comfortably

Sit up straight and keep your wrists and shoulders relaxed.

And three more

  • Aim for accuracy first. Fixing mistakes costs more time than typing a little slower.
  • Read a word or two ahead, so you always know what comes next.
  • Practise for a few minutes every day. Short, regular sessions work best.
